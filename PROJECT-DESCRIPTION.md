## The elevator pitch (spoken, ~30 seconds)

MY UNIVERSE is a personal cosmos — a real-time 3D universe where your life is embodied as
celestial bodies. Every planet is a memory, a person, a dream, a project. Each planet hosts a
living diary, and every entry you write becomes a moon orbiting that planet; the mood of your
words feeds the aurora of your sun. At the edge of your system floats **Eventide**, a
physically-accurate black hole — and inside it, an encrypted vault: a complete filesystem with
a code editor, a terminal, and sandboxed code execution, sealed with Argon2id and AES-256.
Beyond the stars, parallel realities hang as glass marbles in a navigable multiverse, and
deleted worlds rest in a real recycle bin instead of vanishing. None of the numbers are fake —
Kepler orbits, real stellar temperatures, relativistic time dilation. It runs entirely offline,
in the browser and as a desktop app. The thesis of the whole thing: *attention is gravity —
what you look upon begins to orbit you.*

## For developers (technical, ~20 seconds)

MY UNIVERSE (Chasin Love World) is an offline-first personal universe app: React 18 + strict
TypeScript over raw Three.js with hand-written GLSL (no react-three-fiber), a custom observable
store, and a real-physics layer — Kepler solver, Stefan–Boltzmann temperatures, Roche limits,
Schwarzschild geometry — across ~43k lines. It ships a geodesic raymarched black hole ported
verbatim from the `webgpu-black-hole` reference, an Argon2id + AES-GCM-256 encrypted
copy-on-write filesystem with sandboxed JS/Python/HTML/PDF/ISO executors, a Tauri 2 desktop
shell with a compiled C++ physics core, and a disk-mirrored multiverse of path-locked reality
packs. Zero backend, zero cloud — the client owns all truth.

## The soul of it (for the moments that deserve more)

Files, notes and records are normally cold rows in rectangular folders. MY UNIVERSE refuses
that: your memories orbit a star you double-click to enter; your unfinished questions are
planets still falling inward; the vault where you keep your secrets *beats* inside a black
hole because a secret should have an event horizon. It is not a game with a score — it is an
instrument for keeping a life, built with the physics of the real one.
