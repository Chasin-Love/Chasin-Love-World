## 2. Fixes shipped

### Fix 1 — Isolation (leak closed)
- `git mv src/realities/testWorld → src/realities/bin/testWorld` (recoverable, out of the live multiverse; the bin glob skips it).
- `galaxyGenerator.ts`: non-home systems now draw **archetype palettes from their own seeded roll** (already the case for bodies; verified), and the nebula name is drawn from a seeded pool (`Veil, Wisp, Cradle, Shroud, Halo, Mantle, Shimmer`) — no more identical labels across realities.
- Verified live: a new reality's vault starts **empty (0 files)** while Sol Prime keeps all its files; worlds are unique per reality.

### Fix 2 — Every control panel button is real + luxury glass
- `CosmicLineageModal.tsx`: all 11 stage buttons now do something real. Cluster/System keep their **real creation engines**; the other 9 stages became **"Fly to this stage"** — they map the rung to the engine's 11-stage dial ladder (`zoomToHierarchy`), auto-warping to the lineage's reality first when it belongs to another continuum. New prop `onZoomToStage(stageIndex)` wired at both App call sites.
- `index.css` — **Luxury pass (Audi matrix-light design language)** on top of the Quantum Glass system:
  - Rim light rebuilt as **two precise opposing beams** (white-gold signature + accent) sweeping a mostly-dark rim at 14s — matrix-LED discipline instead of a rainbow loop.
  - **Aurora under-glow**: two ambient accent pools baked into the plate's background (no extra DOM/blur planes).
  - Deeper glass: layered gradients, stronger inner top highlight, deeper ambient shadow; warmer gold-white button hover signature; premium 3px tile lift.
  - The live animated canvas backdrop (starfield + rotating cosmic-web spheres + tachyon beams) from wave 4 stays.

### Fix 3 — Creation obeys exactly
- `CreateRealityModal.tsx`: `galaxyCount` **default = 1** (was 4). The live preview line ("1 galaxy · 1 stellar system · …") is now guaranteed truthful.
- `galaxyGenerator.ts`: non-home stellar systems build a **fixed 5-world roster** (deterministic per galaxy id) instead of a random 3–9 roll.
- Cluster count already follows the requested galaxy count (`realities/index.ts:170`) — verified.

### Fix 4 — The reload storm (the big one)
Three layers of defense:
1. **`vite.config.js`**: `server.watch.ignored: ["**/src/realities/**"]` — the dev watcher no longer full-page-reloads when reality modules are written at runtime. New disk realities go live on next boot; the running session keeps its in-state `customRealities` copy (which is already the source of truth for the session).
2. **`server/index.ts` create-folder**: **write-dedup** — renders the module text and compares before writing; identical re-requests (double-submit, retry-queue + live call) now return `deduped: true` without touching the filesystem at all.
3. **`server/realityDaemon.ts`**: **grace window** (`markRecentlyWritten`, 20s) — the 3s auto-repair never rewrites a folder the app just authored, killing the second reload trigger (also stops the documented "daemon clobbers hand-edits" behavior for fresh writes).
