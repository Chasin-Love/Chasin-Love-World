# ROUND 80 — THE MARK (2026-09-30)

> The placeholder dies. The app finally wears its own cosmos on its face:
> the icon, the taskbar, the browser tab — all of it Eventide at rest.

## What happened

The author's complaint: the app icon was "too nasty to look at." True — it was
the foundation-era placeholder (a teal/violet disc generated by
`scripts/make-icons.mjs` "so the build is unblocked"). It was never artwork.

## The mark

`scripts/make-icons.mjs` now draws THE MARK: **Eventide at rest** — a black
event-horizon pit wearing its accretion ring, tilted toward the viewer like an
orbit seen from above. Pure analytic per-pixel rendering (supersampled 4×4 at
small sizes, 2× at large), no image dependencies, same self-contained spirit
as the placeholder it replaces.

The palette is the app's own contract colors, so the mark belongs to the
cosmos it opens:

- **abyss square** `#010208` — the void behind everything, corners kept (it IS the void)
- **white-hot teal** inner edge (`rgb(168,255,240)`) → **teal-ice** (`#42e0ce`) body → **gold fringe** fading outward
- **violet lens halo** (`#8b5cf6`) inside and around the ring — the Kamui's color
- the **near side** (bottom) burns hotter, the far side cools toward violet — Doppler beaming, in miniature
- a hair-thin **photon ring** hugs the horizon; the pit stays true void

Craft notes from the two failed drafts kept in history: v1 blew out to white
(a units bug — 0–255 values run through a 0–1 clamp, pinned here so it never
returns), and v2 washed pastel because the teal→solar blend spent too long in
the milky mid-ramp. v3 compresses the ramp (hot → teal by t=0.32, solar by
t=1.0) and drops the streak frequency to 9 so supersampling doesn't moiré.

## Release ritual (learned this session, written down)

Releases do not happen on the laptop and never needed to. The pipeline
(`.github/workflows/desktop.yml`) builds on GitHub's Windows runner (MSVC
present) on every `v*` tag push, generates `latest.json`, and publishes the
release. The previous session's "cannot build" was a local-toolchain dead end
that never mattered. The v15.0.4 intro-loop fix proved the ritual; v15.0.5
repeats it: bump `tauri.conf.json` + `Cargo.toml` (+ `Cargo.lock`) → commit →
tag `v15.0.5` → push. The updater card delivers the rest.

Housekeeping fixed in passing: `Cargo.toml` had drifted to 15.0.2 while
`tauri.conf.json` rode at 15.0.4 — both now move in lockstep at 15.0.5.
