## 1. KAMUI — the canon (Naruto)

### 1.1 The technique
- **Kamui (神威)** — Obito Uchiha's Mangekyō Sharingan space–time ninjutsu (Kakashi carries the same eye, so he has it too). It **transfers objects (and people) to and from another dimension**.
- **The canonical visual (this is the sentence everything hangs on):** Kamui's transfers are *"often characterised by a **spiralling void that targets swirl into or out of**, **distorting their form** as they move between dimensions."* The distortion is applied to the target itself — matter twists and spirals as it crosses.
- Anything inside Kamui's dimension is **untraceable** — its chakra cannot be detected by any means in the normal world. The dimension is also a **refuge** and a **prison**: whoever is sent there cannot leave on their own.
- Objects stored in the dimension can later be **ejected with force** — Kamui is also a weapon (Obito's stored shuriken fly back out at attack speed).
- The only known ways in or out are the two eyes. Obito and Kakashi are immune to each other's Kamui (they can simply teleport themselves away mid-warp) and can each block the other's warps on third parties.

### 1.2 The right eye — short range (Obito)
- Warps **himself and anything he is touching** into the dimension. He does not need to pre-designate a destination — he can emerge anywhere he pleases, which the story treats as *superior to the Flying Thunder God technique*.
- Usable on other people too, normally requiring **physical contact**, and faster to use on others than on himself. He can also pull others in through the eye itself.
- **Intangibility (the signature trick):** he *automatically transfers only the parts of his body that would have been touched* into the dimension the instant something passes through him — so attacks phase through him. Everything in contact with him (clothes, held weapons, even other people he grabs) becomes intangible along with him. He can phase through barriers, extend attacks through walls, and "sink" into the ground.
- **Costs / limits of the right eye:**
  - He **cannot interact with the world** while intangible, and must consciously re-solidify to attack.
  - Intangibility can only be sustained **~5 minutes** continuously.
  - He **cannot teleport while intangible** — both use the same transfer — leaving a vulnerable gap at the moment he switches from phasing to warping.
  - His counter: anything that is *already inside* the dimension (or sent there by the left eye) can hit him while he phases. The left eye is his natural predator.

### 1.3 The left eye — long range (Kakashi)
- The user **looks at a target** and creates a **"barrier space"** around it, whose **size is determined by the user**.
- *"When the user concentrates, **anything within the barrier is pulled toward its centre**, transferring the target to Kamui's dimension."* — i.e. the long-range form is literally a **suction vortex**: a bounded region of space with a defined capture radius, whose contents are drawn spiralling into a center point until they cross over.
- Accuracy and barrier control are a skill: early Kakashi struggled to warp a moving target's *chosen body part*; later he warps projectiles **mid-flight** (a nail into Obito's shoulder, a kunai through Madara's chest) and can warp himself (barrier himself) to teleport.
- **Costs / limits of the left eye:** for a non-Uchiha the chakra cost is enormous — initially ~3 uses/day, the eye bleeds with repeated use, and his eyesight degrades. Nine-Tails chakra makes it ~3× more powerful, faster, and able to swallow targets as large as a tailed beast's head.
- Defence against long-range Kamui is said to be **impossible** — *except* for the right-eye counterpart (self-teleport out of the barrier).

### 1.4 Both eyes together
- Using both eyes **doubles the warp speed** and allows generating **large, stable portals** that other people can travel through (used to extract Sasuke from Madara's dimension).
- The eyes can **synchronize with other space–time ninjutsu** to reach dimensions Kamui alone cannot connect to — evidence that Kamui can link *totally isolated* spaces when boosted.

### 1.5 Kamui's Dimension (神威の時空間, "Kamui's time–space")
- An **alternate space created by the Mangekyō** — a completely separate time–space with **no natural connection** to the normal world. This is exactly the "dimension A / dimension B with no possible bridge" framing.
- Its shown landscape: a **desolate, arid desert-like terrain** under a dim sky, scattered with **rocky ruins/debris** — a dead, empty universe.
- Named events set there: Obito warps Sasuke & Karin there after the Five Kage Summit; the Kakashi-vs-Obito duel happens inside it (their two Kamuis interacting in the same space); Naruto & Sakura are stashed there; Sasuke is pulled out of Madara's separate dimension by synchronized Kamui.

### 1.6 The eye that names the effect
- Obito's and Kakashi's Mangekyō are **identical** (same eye): a **three-bladed pinwheel whose blades curl like spiralling commas** — a triangle of tomoe-swirls spinning around the pupil.
- The **tomoe (巴)** is a real Japanese swirl symbol (magatama bead; three tomoe = pinwheel crest of Hachiman, god of war). Narutopedia: all Mangekyō designs *"resemble pinwheels."*
- Thematic rhyme: Obito wears the **spiral Zetsu mask**, calls himself "Tobi," and his whole visual identity is the **spiral** — because his power *is* a spiral. The Kamui motif = concentric, pinwheel-swirling rotation.
- **Name meaning:** Kamui is the divine being in **Ainu mythology** — read in Japanese as *"the might/majesty of the gods."* The effect should feel like a divine rupture, not a machine door.

### 1.7 How the anime actually presents it (visual read)
- **Self-teleport / phase:** a rapid **spiral ripple** blooms out of (or collapses into) the eye/body — concentric, rotating rings, blue-black with a light rim, over in a fraction of a second. When Obito sinks into the ground, the surface swirls around him like liquid.
- **Long-range warp:** a **barrier circle appears around the target**, and the target **twists, stretches and spirals inward**, shrinking toward the barrier's center while the whole region rotates; the last thing seen is the target smeared into the swirl. The *form of the target* distorts — it does not simply shrink and vanish.
- **Ejection:** the void opens and matter **spins back out** along the same spiral, in reverse.
- Speed read: Kamui is framed as one of the fastest space–time techniques in the setting — the suck is *fast*: grab → swirl → gone in about a second. The swirl is short, violent, and decisive, not a slow drain.
- Fan VFX recreations (e.g. After Effects breakdowns) all converge on the same recipe: **spiral displacement mask + radial motion blur + rotating concentric rings + chromatic aberration at the rim**, converging on the eye.

## 2. BLACK HOLE PHYSICS — the real mechanism behind the look

### 2.1 Why "suction" is the wrong picture — and the right one
- A black hole is not a vacuum cleaner; it's **geometry**. Mass curves spacetime, and everything (light included) simply follows the curved lines. What looks like "sucking" is matter **falling along curved space**, losing altitude (potential) and orbit.
- **Conservation of angular momentum is why the vortex exists.** Matter almost never falls straight in: it arrives with sideways motion, orbits, and can only spiral inward if it *sheds angular momentum*. So infalling matter forms a **whirling disk** — the accretion disk — that slowly drains inward over many orbits. **The swirl is not decoration; it IS the physics.**
- **Differential rotation:** inner gas orbits much faster than outer gas (Keplerian, roughly ω ∝ r^−3/2). The disk constantly shears — inner rings lap outer rings — which winds any structure into **trailing spiral streaks**. That shear is what gives the vortex its "liquid, smeared" look.
- **Angular momentum transport & heating:** friction alone is too weak; **magnetic turbulence (the magnetorotational instability, MRI)** does the work — and converting orbital energy into heat makes disk gas reach **millions of degrees**, glowing X-ray white-hot at the inner edge and cooler (infrared, duller) at the rim. Visually: **color temperature rises toward the core** — outer swirl deep red/orange, inner edge white/blue.
- **ISCO (innermost stable circular orbit):** there is a hard inner edge where stable orbits end; inside it matter **plunges** rather than spirals. So the swirl has a *defined inner rim*, then a sudden dark gap (the plunge region), then the shadow. Real disks don't smear into the hole — they *end* at a bright inner edge.

### 2.2 Sizes and silhouettes (the geometry of the hole)
- **Event horizon** at the Schwarzschild radius R_s — the point of no return (escape velocity = c).
- **Photon sphere** at **1.5 R_s** — light itself can orbit there (unstably). 
- **The shadow** the hole casts on the sky is much bigger than the horizon: apparent radius ≈ **2.6 R_s** (impact parameter b = 3√3/2 · R_s), because light passing near the photon sphere is bent into it. Visually: a **black core wrapped in a thin, brilliant ring** (the photon ring), everything else lensed around it.
- **Gravitational lensing = "the universe surface bending."** Background starlight is deflected around the hole; directly behind it, stars smear into an **Einstein ring**; the accretion disk's far side is lifted and bent so it appears **above and below** the shadow (the Interstellar halo). A strong vortex should visibly **warp, streak and drag the background starfield tangentially** around it — the sky itself participates in the swirl.
- **Doppler beaming:** disk plasma orbits at near-light speed; the side rotating *toward* the viewer is dramatically brighter (and bluer), the receding side dimmer and redder. (The EHT's M87* image is bright on exactly one side for this reason.)
- **Gravitational redshift:** light from close to the horizon loses energy — the inner edge darkens/reddens as well as brightens, giving the characteristic "hot but dying" rim.
- **Relativistic jets (optional flourish):** some accreting holes launch beams of plasma from the poles along the spin axis — a vertical lance out of the vortex, if the moment needs one.

### 2.3 Spaghettification — "the anchor star lost its structure"
- **Tidal force** is the *gradient* of gravity across an object: F ∝ 1/**r³**. Because it scales with the cube of distance, tidal destruction arrives **suddenly and close in** — long before any uniform "pull" would tear things.
- Falling feet-first: you are **stretched radially** (feet pulled harder than head) and **compressed transversely** (sides squeezed inward) — volume conserved, shape noodled. Stephen Hawking popularized the term; Hawking's astronaut, "Death by Black Hole" (Tyson) are the classic tellings.
- **Mass decides where it happens:** around a **stellar-mass** hole, tidal shredding happens *outside* the horizon (you're spaghetti before you cross); around a **supermassive** hole, the horizon comes first and you cross intact (destruction waits inside). For the project: small cosmic bodies shred visibly *outside* their core; huge ones swallow things whole and clean.
- **Tidal disruption event (TDE) — the real "anchor star loses structure" event:** when a star wanders within the **tidal radius**, it is **deformed and shredded into thin streams**:
  - the debris splits into a **bound tail** (falls back, wraps around the hole, circularizes into a glowing disk — the flare) and an **unbound tail** (~half the star's mass) ejected at up to **~10,000 km/s**;
  - the fallback luminosity decays on the famous **t ∗ rˆ−5/3** law — bright flare, slow fade;
  - observed in real time: **AT 2019qiz** (ESO, 2020) — the star's atmosphere stretched and compressed into spaghetti streams before being consumed.
  - Visual recipe of a TDE: a **bright elongated stream** peels off the star, wraps into an elliptical spiral around the core, part of it whips away outward, the rest heats up and feeds the swirl. *(Your image #1 — star glowing at the center of a dark spiral — is almost exactly the artist renders of this event.)*

### 2.4 Frame dragging — spacetime itself in swirling motion
- A **rotating** black hole (Kerr) doesn't just curve spacetime — it **drags it around**, forcing nearby space to **co-rotate** (Lense–Thirring effect). Orbits, gyroscopes, disks — everything precesses and is swept along the spin.
- **Ergosphere:** between the horizon and the static limit, spacetime is dragged so hard that **nothing can possibly stand still** — co-rotation is mandatory. If any region of the effect should have a hard "you WILL rotate" rule, this is its real-physics justification: near the activated body, *reality itself is in mandatory swirling motion*, independent of the objects in it.
- 2025 observationally: wobbling X-ray emission from a feeding hole matched Lense–Thirring precession — the universe's confirmation that the swirl is in the *fabric*, not just the matter.
- **The philosophical gift:** frame dragging means "the background/reality bending and swirling" is not fantasy license — it is precisely what strong rotating gravity does. The **universe surface** (their background starfield) swirling *before* the objects move is the physically-correct order of events.

## 3. THE VISUAL LANGUAGE — how films and games actually build it

### 3.1 Interstellar's Gargantua (the gold standard)
- Built by **Double Negative with physicist Kip Thorne** using **DNGR** ("Double Negative Gravitational Renderer"), which **ray-traces light through curved spacetime** (solves ray-bundle transport in the Kerr metric) instead of faking lensing. Published in *Classical and Quantum Gravity* (James, von Tunzelmann, Franklin & Thorne, 2015); Guinness recognizes it as the most scientifically accurate movie black hole.
- What it produced: the disk's far side lensed into the **vertical halo above/below** the shadow; a **thin photon ring** hugging the silhouette; multiple nested images of the disk.
- **Deliberate inaccuracy for beauty:** physically, Doppler beaming should make one side of the disk searingly bright and blue-white. Nolan had it **toned down / made symmetric** so audiences wouldn't read it as bad lighting. Lesson: **physical truth first, then bend one rule for legibility.**
- The renderer even revealed real phenomena (unexpected halo structure), and hid the easter egg "aaargh" in a message decoded from its output.

### 3.2 The EHT real images (what the universe actually renders)
- **M87\*** (2019): a glowing asymmetric ring around a dark shadow — **6.5 billion solar masses**; brightness asymmetry = relativistic beaming of plasma circling near light speed. **Sagittarius A\*** (2022): same architecture, our own galaxy.
- The ring is the **photon ring**: light that has orbited the hole one or more times before escaping. Its size is proportional to mass; the next-generation EHT aims to image it directly.

### 3.3 The game-VFX consensus recipe (layer stack)
How the effect is typically composed in real-time engines (Unity Shader Graph / VFX Graph / Unreal, per RealTimeVFX and tutorial breakdowns):
1. **Black core** — pure black sphere/disc; *nothing* renders inside it.
2. **Photon-ring rim** — thin, hot fresnel ring hugging the core (often with slight chromatic aberration).
3. **Accretion disk** — annular plane/billboard with **polar-coordinate UVs**; layered scrolling noise; **differential rotation** (inner UV ring rotates faster than outer); color ramp from dull outer → white-hot inner edge; additive blending; optional Doppler brightness on one side.
4. **Screen-space swirl / lensing pass** — the money layer for "reality bends": the background's UVs are **rotated around the vortex center with a strength that falls off with distance**, plus a **radial pull inward** (1/r²-flavored falloff from the suction center). Stars near the vortex **smear tangentially** — the universe surface literally warps.
5. **In-spiraling particles** — spawn at the capture radius with tangential velocity + inward attraction, leaving motion-streaked trails; denser and faster near the core.
6. **Target deformation** — objects being swallowed are **stretched along the radial direction and twisted tangentially** (spaghettification + shear) before vanishing at the rim.
7. **Chromatic aberration + shake** — a thin rainbow fringe around the vortex (their screenshot #4 already shows this) sells the "light is being bent" story.
- Suction-center pattern (dbbd.sg breakdown): define a **world-space origin + falloff radius**; all distortion, particle forces and object pull read from that one center — exactly the "concentrated gravitational force stored in the clicked body" idea: **one center, one capture radius, everything else falls off with distance.**

### 3.4 Vortex math that draws the right shape
- **Rankine vortex** (the whirlpool model): a **solid-body-rotating core** (everything spins together, v ∝ r) transitioning to an **outer free vortex** (v ∝ 1/r). Result: the center rotates like a solid disc, the outskirts trail off — the classic eye-of-the-storm look with a fast core and lazy rim.
- **Logarithmic spiral** r = a·e^(bθ): the natural arm shape of differential rotation and of galaxies (the Whirlpool Galaxy M51's arms are the poster child; real galaxies avoid the "winding problem" via density waves — pattern vs material). Any "arm" drawn as a log spiral instantly reads as cosmic.
- **Trailing arms:** because the inside rotates faster, spiral arms must *trail behind* the rotation direction (curl backward relative to spin). Getting the handedness right is the single biggest realism tell in vortex art.
- **Spirographic orbits** (Phys. Rev. Fluids 2022): particles in Rankine-like vortices trace nested rosette/spirograph loops — a subtle detail: infalling debris doesn't just spiral, it **oscillates radially while spiraling**, making rosette paths.

### 3.5 The tear / portal moment
- Layered construction (RealTimeVFX breakdowns, e.g. "Sketch #22" — collapse tears a wormhole in space): **implosion phase → a beat of stillness/darkness → radial energy release → the tear opens as a bright crack/ring → stabilized into a swirl-rimmed void**.
- Kamui's specific flavor: the portal is **not** a glowing fantasy gate — it is a **dark, spiralling void** (the same motif as the eye: a pinwheel of dark concentric swirls), matter exits *spinning*. The hole and the portal are **the same object**: the vortex's terminal point IS the door. Suction is the door opening slowly; the tear is the door opening all the way.
- Reinforcement cues used across tutorials: a **radial shockwave/refraction ring** at the instant of tearing, background briefly **streaks radially outward** (release after inrush), sound/pacing of a sharp inhale then silence.

## 4. SYNTHESIS — mapping it onto the project's Kamui concept

The concept as described: *activating Kamui wakes the concentrated gravitational force stored inside a clicked cosmic body; everything around — background universe surface, nearby stars, nearby planets, other cosmic bodies, reality itself — bends and enters swirling motion, sucked toward the body's core; nearby bodies temporarily lose their structure and stream toward the core; at the end, the swirl opens a portal to another dimension (totally isolated, or same plane very far away).*

The research says this concept is not just cool — it is **almost a faithful physics simulation** if staged in this order:

| Phase | The concept | The real-physics anchor | The Kamui canon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Awakening | "Concentrated gravitational force stored in every cosmic body activates" | Mass curves spacetime; the body's gravity was always there, now it **deepens** (a gravity well suddenly forms) | Mangekyō **activates** — the eye's pinwheel spins up |
| 1 — Reality bends first | "The background / universe surface bends in swirling motion" | **Frame dragging**: rotating gravity drags space itself — *the fabric moves before the objects do*; lensing smears background stars tangentially | The **spiral ripple** blooming from the eye |
| 2 — The whirlpool | "Everything nearby is sucked, swirling toward the core" | **Angular momentum conservation**: nothing falls straight in; infall = inspiral; **differential rotation** → trailing log-spiral streaks; **Rankine vortex** velocity profile | Long-range Kamui = a **barrier space** with user-defined capture radius; *"anything within the barrier is pulled toward its centre"* |
| 3 — Structure loss | "The anchor star temporarily loses its structure, sucked toward the core" | **Tidal disruption**: F ∝ 1/r³ → star stretches into streams (spaghettification); bound tail wraps into the disk, unbound tail flung out; brightens as it feeds | Kamui *"distorts the form"* of what it takes — targets twist, not shrink |
| 4 — The core look | The vortex's center | **Shadow ≈ 2.6 R_s**, thin **photon ring** at 1.5 R_s, hot inner edge ends abruptly at the **ISCO**; Doppler-bright side optional; color heat gradient inward | The void is **dark with a swirling rim** — the eye's pinwheel motif |
| 5 — The tear | "It opens what's inside — a portal to dimension B" | Wormhole/tear pacing: implosion → beat of silence → radial release → opening | Kamui **is** the crossing: the swirl's terminal point is the door; ejection reverses the same spiral |
| 6 — The rule set | — | Falloff with distance from ONE suction center; capture radius = hard edge of the barrier | Contact-based (right eye) vs gaze/barrier-based (left eye); fast (~1 s); immune/challenge rules between two Kamui users; contents untraceable; ~5 min sustain limit |

**Design truths the research keeps repeating:**
1. **The swirl is the teleport.** In both canon and physics, the vortex and the portal are one object — suction is just the door opening gradually. Don't design "vortex, then separate portal": design the vortex *ending* as the portal.
2. **Space moves first.** Frame dragging + lensing say the background bends before the stars move. The "universe surface" warping is phase 1, not garnish.
3. **Nothing falls straight.** Every particle, star and planet should arrive on a curved, trailing path. Straight-line suction reads as cheap vacuum cleaner; curved inspiral reads as gravity.
4. **The target deforms.** Canon says "distorting their form"; physics says spaghettify. Whatever is swallowed should stretch radially and twist tangentially — never just scale down.
5. **Hard inner edge.** The core is not a blurry hole: a dark shadow with a **thin bright ring** and an abrupt end of the disk (ISCO) — precision at the center sells the gravity.
6. **One bright side (optional but true).** Doppler beaming: rotate the disk so one side glows brighter/bluer. Interstellar removed it for clarity; the EHT says the universe has it. Choose per mood.
7. **Speed is character.** Kamui's suck lasts about a second — violent, decisive. A slow drain changes the personality of the technique entirely (that's "black hole ambience", not Kamui).
8. **The eye motif is the brand.** Three spiralling pinwheel blades = the Mangekyō design = the tomoe. The vortex, the portal rim, the activation ripple can all be variations of one spiral motif — that is exactly how Obito's identity is built in the anime.
