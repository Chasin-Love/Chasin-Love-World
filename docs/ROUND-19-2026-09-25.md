# ROUND 19 — THE REAL SPACETIME: a ported geodesic black hole

**Date:** 2026-09-25 · **Build:** R50 · **Status:** SHIPPED

## What was asked

> "Port dgreenheck/webgpu-black-hole into the project as-is, just smaller
> overall — the hole is not the center of the project. But the rest of all
> should be same — especially the spacetime bending at every tangential
> point. Use the open-source code directly, don't re-derive it."

The "bending at every tangential point" is the thing every painted fake
cannot do: the far side of the disk lifted into an unbroken ring **around**
the shadow, its secondary image beneath, the halo continuous at every angle
because it is **integrated**, not drawn. His code does exactly this. So we
took his code.

## The port

Source: https://github.com/dgreenheck/webgpu-black-hole — `blackhole-shader.js`
(TSL/WGSL) + `blackhole.js` (parameters). **MIT License © 2025 Daniel
Greenheck.** Attribution owed and given: the file header of
`src/engine/blackholeRaymarch.ts` names him, the license, and this document.

What was transliterated from his TSL to GLSL, 1:1:

| His code | Ours |
|---|---|
| `Loop(64)` geodesic march, step 0.3 rs | same integrator (128-step budget — see bugs) |
| bend `a = −rs/r²`, capture `r < 1.01 rs`, escape `r > 100` | identical |
| disk hit on EVERY plane crossing, front-to-back `remaining = 1−α` compositing | identical — **the continuous halo, by construction** |
| Mitchell–Charity blackbody table, 1000–10000 K @ 100 K | identical 91-entry table → LUT texture |
| `T(r) = T_peak·(r_in/r)^0.75`, Doppler `D = 1/(1−β·cosθ)`, brightness ∝ D³, clamp [0.1, 5] | identical |
| edge softness 0.15/0.15, turbulence stretch 5, cycle 10 s, ω ∝ r^−1.5 | identical |
| `pow(color, 1/2.2)` gamma | identical |

Dropped on purpose: his procedural starfield/nebulae — **our** universe (the
photo sky, the lensed reality) is the background, so escaped rays exit
transparent and the real sky lenses behind the hole.

Scale: his demo pinned the camera ~10 rs out; ours is one body among many,
so everything marches in rs units and the renderer is identical at any hole
size. Options interface: `{ intensity?, steps? }`.

## The four bugs the verification gauntlet caught

The port was written once, then **verified by execution**, not by hope:

1. **LUT coordinate ×90.** `u = (t−1000)·0.01·90+0.5)/91` clamped every
   temperature above ~1100 K to the bluest texel. Found by diffing the
   lookup against his source; the TS mirror was right, only the GLSL was
   wrong. Fixed to `(t−1000)·0.01`.
2. **March origin.** His march starts at the camera — fine at ~10 rs,
   catastrophic at scene scale (64×0.3 = 19.2 rs of budget spent
   commuting; the disk would never appear). First fix (start at fragment)
   was wrong the other way: it skips the disk's near face, which lies
   *between* the camera and the billboard quad. Final fix: a **coarse
   straight approach** (≤12 steps down to r=10, crossings still composited
   — the leg is straight so they are exact) hands off to his untouched
   integrator inside.
3. **Captured-ray contract.** I had captured rays exit transparent to let
   the composite sphere own the black; his contract returns accumulated
   foreground disk light, **fully opaque**. Transparent capture opens a
   sky-colored ring between the composite silhouette and the geodesic
   capture cone, and deletes near-side disk light crossing the shadow.
   Matched his contract exactly; the composite stays underneath as the
   infallible fallback.
4. **Double-alpha blending.** The march accumulates premultiplied color
   (`rgb·α` per hit); plain NormalBlending would multiply alpha again —
   the R47 "mud" regression in miniature. `premultipliedAlpha: true`
   set to match the accumulation.

Also caught live: `uLensing` missing from the uniform block (compile error
on GPU), declarations-after-code (GLSL C89 rule), and his velocity
convention living in the disk-local frame (photon direction transformed by
`uDiskBasis` before the Doppler dot).

## The safety contract (unchanged)

This is an overlay on the infallible composite. If the shader ever fails
to compile, the `eventide-shader-error` hook hides it, `setCinematic(false)`
restores the baked disk + arcs, and the hole survives. The geodesic tier
owns the disk only when it proves itself alive — `engine.ts` calls
`setCinematic?.(true)` at all three attach sites (regular build, cinematic
path, initial-profile path).
