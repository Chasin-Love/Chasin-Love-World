## Part A — the real lens law on the universe surface

`surfaceShaders.ts` gained **hole mode** (`uLensStrong[16]`):

- Sampled image angle θ maps through the **exact thin-lens image equation**
  β = θ − θ_E²/θ with θ_E² = 2·rs_ang (rs_ang = 0.62·rim — always the size of
  the hole itself, per frame from its true radius & distance).
- **Second-order term** (15π/16)(rs/θ)² tightens the bend near the critical
  angle instead of falling off linearly.
- **Capture**: image angles below b_c = (3√3/2)·rs_ang return a sentinel —
  no image exists there — and the dome fragment paints **true black**
  (normal blending, a real hole in the luminous sky): the EHT look,
  produced by the equation rather than painted on.
- **Fold-safety is proven by the math**: dβ/dθ = 1 + (θ_E²−2c·rs²)/θ² ≥ 0.42 > 0
  on the imaged domain, and β(b_c) ≈ 1.39 rs > 0 — the forward map cannot
  fold at any distance for any hole. Round 14's no-flat-disc guarantee is now
  a theorem, not a clamp.
- Star shells and nebulae inherit everything (shared GLSL): background stars
  visibly stream around the hole and vanish crossing the shadow.
- Stars and planets keep the proven Round-14 weak law (halo always the size
  of the body's own silhouette).
- The damped **Lens · Bent/Clear** toggle gates capture: *Clear* releases
  the sky whole (verified on-GPU).

## Part D — the stability contract (the owner's explicit worry)

- **Lensing is purely optical.** It bends light on the background canvas and
  star shells; it adds no force to any body. The universe's dynamics never
  felt the hole's lens because the lens is not a force.
- **The Dimensional Anchor** (Round 14) still holds: the vault's 10 M☉
  dynamic reach is tempered to 10⁻³; its *lens* bends like the true
  singularity it is. Einstein keeps the truth, Newton keeps the peace.
- **Living Gravity** remains bounded-state (angles + fenced scalars, Keeper
  at e ≤ 0.6, canonical heal one toggle away) — the torture-tested
  no-ejection guarantee is untouched.
- New shader code rides the existing safety contracts: the raymarch overlay
  is additive and disarmable; the surface bend is fold-safe by construction.

Plus one honest number in the **Physics HUD** (GR card): the shadow diameter
⌀ = 3√3·GM/c² beside the existing Schwarzschild radius.

## Design law (extends Round 14's)

**The bodies never bend; the universe surface does — and where the surface
is in contact with a black hole, the equations of 1915/1959/2019 decide what
light does.** No painted shadows, no fake rings: if it renders, it is because
a geodesic says so. The shadow is black because no image exists there.
