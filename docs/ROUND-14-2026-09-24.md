# Round 14 — Gravitational Lensing & Living Gravity (2026-09-24)

The universe simulated real physics as *telemetry* — Kepler periods, Newton's
F = G·m₁·m₂/r², Schwarzschild radii — but Einstein's curvature itself was
invisible. Round 14 makes it observable the only way it can be observed in a
real universe: **light bending around mass**, plus true mutual N-body gravity
that can **never** destabilize the diary worlds.

## Addendum — Gravitational Lensing of the Universe Surface (R39)

**The owner's three laws (learned across two corrections):**
1. **The bodies never bend.** The anchor star, the planets, every cosmic body
   stays perfectly rigid — no exceptions.
2. **Curvature lives on the background — the surface of the universe.** The
   first build drew a rubber-sheet lattice (removed: *the real universe has
   no grid*); the second bent the rendered image around each body
   (removed: it warped the bodies' own edges). Einstein's curvature is only
   observable in the *background light* — so that is the only place it is
   drawn.
3. **The attraction is real, its visible effect on bodies is nullified.**
   Mutual gravitational attraction acts between the worlds, but orbital
   motion balances it — no body visually warps another. Living Gravity
   (R40) models exactly that attraction; the lensing draws nothing on
   bodies.

**Final architecture — `surfaceShaders.ts`:**

- `applyLensBend()` bends the sampled direction on the **universe surface** —
  the inverted celestial dome — with the true thin-lens law: deflection
  α = θ_E²/θ, so background features appear displaced away from each mass
  (starlight arcs around it — the 1919 eclipse measurement). Applied before
  any canvas layer is sampled.
- The three background **star shells** (deep nebulae, far stars, neighborhood
  stars) bend per-vertex through the same shared lens uniforms — background
  stars visibly migrate around the masses.
- Per frame, the engine feeds each body's direction-from-camera and its
  **Einstein-ring angle θ_E** (`lensStrengthFor`, log-compressed real mass:
  worlds 0.014–0.036 rad, anchor star 0.05 rad ≈ 2.9°, Eventide Vault
  0.115 rad ≈ 6.6° — strongest), up to 16 lenses. Damped toggle
  (`setSpacetimeLens`).

## Addendum 2 — Living Gravity, N-body coupling that cannot destabilize (R40)

**`src/physics/nbody.ts`** — every world now gravitationally attracts every
other world, in the formalism real celestial mechanics uses for exactly this
problem:

- **Osculating elements, not position deltas.** A first attempt integrating
  position offsets against the prescribed Kepler ephemeris (the Encke-style
  shortcut) was built, tested, and **rejected by our own verification**: the
  tidal tensor's anti-restoring directions pump the delta secularly, and
  within two centuries every world was pinned against a damping fence. Real
  perturbation theory instead integrates the ORBITAL ELEMENTS with **Gauss's
  planetary equations** (Murray & Dermott, *Solar System Dynamics*) — the
  machinery behind Lagrange–Laplace secular theory and every modern
  long-term Solar System model (Laskar). The dominant star–planet Kepler
  problem stays solved *exactly* in closed form; each world's eccentricity,
  apsidal angle and inclination breathe and precess under the real mutual
  tides; the rendered position is re-derived from the exact Kepler solver of
  the osculating elements. Positions are only ever *evaluated* from bounded
  variables — never integrated.
- **Structural stability (the guarantee).** The integrated state is two
  angles and one clamped scalar: Δe fenced by **THE KEEPER** at 0.6 (plus a
  rate brake and a ±20° inclination fence). There is no drift channel — a
  pathological roster can deform every orbit but can never eject a world.
  Torture test: a 1000 M☉ intruder at 30 scene units ran 200 sim-years —
  orbits deformed wildly, the Keeper engaged throughout, nothing escaped,
  nothing NaN'd. This mirrors why the real Solar System endures: its chaos
  is *bounded element diffusion* (KAM / resonance fencing), never open
  runaway.
- **Democratic-Heliocentric correctness.** The disturbing acceleration
  carries the indirect term (−Σ G′mⱼpⱼ/|pⱼ|³ — the uniform field of the
  star's own accelerating frame), Plummer-softened (ε ≈ 1.8 M km) so close
  encounters stay finite (the Hill-separation role).
- **Canonical heal.** The divine orbital elements are the sole source of
  truth and are never written. Zeroing (Δe, Δω, Δi) — toggling Living
  Gravity off, the *Restore Ephemeris* deck control, or re-syncing a roster —
  restores the exact canonical ephemeris. Destabilization is incapable of
  becoming permanent.
- **The Dimensional Anchor.** The Vault's true 10 M☉ dynamic reach would
  unmake the inner system (a real 10 M☉ body at 4.8 AU ejects everything —
  and the lore already idealizes its stable companionship with a 1 M☉

## Addendum 4 — The Hole in the Sky (R42, final after owner review)

**The owner's concept:** a black hole IS a hole in the surface of the
universe. Look at the reference imagery: where the hole sits, the band of
stars bends in a circular halo hugging its silhouette — **the same realistic
lensing every cosmic body gets, at hole strength** — and the rest of the sky
is untouched. Two invented intermediates (a fixed funnel, then an
elastically-trailing patch) were rejected and removed: no fantasy geometry,
just the honest Einstein bend, concentrated where the surface is in contact
with the hole.

- The Vault rejoined the **generic lens list** and every body's halo is now
  **proportional to its own apparent silhouette** (θ_f = multiplier ×
  asin(R/d), computed per frame — worlds 2.2×, star 2.6×, Vault 3.2×). A
  black hole's disc IS a hollow in the surface of reality: the surface in
  contact with it bends with the thin-lens law, and the halo can never
  dwarf the universe at one distance and vanish at another. The drag is
  anchored to the rim — strongest exactly at the silhouette (stars pile
  into bright arcs at the hole's edge) — decaying with the true 1/θ toward
  the halo edge, and **fold-safe by construction** (the sampled direction
  can never cross the lens center — the flat-disc artifact is impossible).
- The funnel machinery (throat shading, rim glow, spiral drag, BH uniforms)
  was **removed entirely** — the surface carries only the honest lens.
- Directions are read from each body's live `group.position` (never the
  one-frame-stale `matrixWorld`), so the halo is glued to the hole with zero
  lag: when the hole moves, the bend moves with it.
- The background **star shells bend with the same shared uniforms**
  (per-vertex). Strength rides the damped *Lens* toggle — *Lens · Clear*
  releases the sky.
